About Our Club

Welcome to the Technology Student Club! Our club is a community for students who are interested in computers, programming, web development, cybersecurity, and other areas of technology.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help students develop technology skills, explore new ideas, work together on projects, and prepare for future careers in the information technology field. We welcome students of all experience levels.

Benefits of Membership

Joining the Technology Student Club gives students opportunities to learn, grow, and build professional connections.

  • Improve computer and technical skills.
  • Learn HTML, CSS, programming, and web design.
  • Work with other students on technology projects.
  • Develop teamwork and problem-solving skills.
  • Explore careers in information technology.
  • Build projects to include in a professional portfolio.

Upcoming Club Activities

Our club plans activities that help members gain practical experience and share their knowledge.

  1. HTML and CSS Workshop: Practice creating and styling webpages.
  2. Web Design Challenge: Collaborate with other members to build a website.
  3. Cybersecurity Awareness Session: Learn about online safety and digital security.
  4. Technology Career Discussion: Explore career paths and professional development.

Meeting dates and locations will be announced by the club leadership.

Technology Resources for Members

Members can use the following online resources to practice new skills and continue learning outside club meetings.

Learning Web Development

  • MDN HTML Guide - Learn how to structure webpages.
  • W3Schools CSS Tutorial - Learn how to style websites.
  • GitHub - Store projects and practice version control.
  • freeCodeCamp - Practice coding through interactive lessons.
